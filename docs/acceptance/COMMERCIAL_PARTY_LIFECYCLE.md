# EP05-A — Customer and Supplier Lifecycle

## Scope

This bounded unit replaces destructive customer and supplier deletion with a
reversible, audited archive lifecycle. It does not claim completion of the
remaining EP05 product, sales, rental, invoice, or payment lifecycle work.

## Production baseline before migration

- Customers: 2
- Suppliers: 1
- Sales: 2
- Rentals: 0
- Customer receipts: 2
- Supplier payments: 2
- No rows were inserted, updated, archived, restored, or deleted during the
  read-only reconciliation.

## Protected behavior

- Direct deletion of a customer or supplier is rejected by a database trigger.
- Legacy DELETE policies are removed as an additional RLS boundary.
- Archive and restore require the existing `can_delete_rows()` authorization.
- An archive reason is mandatory and the actor is derived from `auth.uid()`.
- The archive timestamp is generated by PostgreSQL, not trusted from the client.
- Archived records cannot be edited until they are restored.
- Archived parties remain readable so historical ledgers and document names do
  not disappear.
- New sales, rentals, receipts, projects, procurement records, payments,
  purchases, budget items, supplier documents, and assets reject archived party
  references in the database.

## User experience

- Active customers and suppliers remain in the primary workspace.
- Archived records move to a collapsed history section with reason and date.
- Managers can restore archived records.
- Archived parties are excluded from selectors for new sales, rentals,
  receipts, projects, supplier payments, purchases, and supplier quotes.
- Existing historical records continue to resolve archived names.
